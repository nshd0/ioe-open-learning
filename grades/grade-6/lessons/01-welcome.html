Lesson 01 — Welcome & Unit Overview

Learning objective: Understand the unit's goals and how projects will be tested.

Time: 20–30 minutes

What you need: Paper, pencils, class whiteboard.

Big idea

Computational thinking helps us break problems into steps that a person or a simple machine can follow.

Try it

In pairs, write step-by-step instructions for making a paper airplane. Swap with another pair and follow their steps exactly. Discuss differences and surprises.

Reflection

What step was unclear? How did you improve it after testing?
