Algorithms — Printable Activity

Grade: 6 • Time: 40–50 minutes • Materials: counters, small objects, paper, pens

Learning objective

Write clear, ordered steps (an algorithm) to solve a simple sorting task and test it for correctness.

Activity (student sheet)

  1. Form groups of 3. Choose a pile of 12 small objects (mix of colours and sizes).
  2. One student (Writer) writes an algorithm with numbered steps to sort the pile by colour then size.
  3. A second student (Runner) follows the written steps exactly to sort the objects. The third (Observer) notes any problems.
  4. Swap roles until each student has been Writer and Runner once.

Testing & Improvement

Observer checklist (tick if yes):

  • Steps were followed without extra help
  • Final pile matched the Writer's intended order
  • Runner asked no clarifying questions

Reflection

What step caused confusion? How would you rewrite it to be clearer?

Teacher: leave space for student response when printing.
