# Lesson 01 — Algorithm Design

Age: 11–12 | Duration: 50 minutes

Learning objectives
- Design clear, testable algorithms for non-trivial tasks.

Lesson flow
- Introduce problem statements and criteria for success; model decomposition.

VARK differentiation
- Visual: Flowcharts and worked examples.
- Auditory: Group discussion and think-aloud modelling.
- Read/Write: Write algorithm descriptions and pseudocode.
- Kinesthetic: Use physical tokens to simulate data flow.

Assessment
- Peer review of algorithm clarity and test cases.
