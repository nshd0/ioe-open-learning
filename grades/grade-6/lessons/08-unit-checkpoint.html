Lesson 08 — Unit Checkpoint

Learning objective: Review and demonstrate learned CT skills by completing a short project and self-assessment.

Time: 60 minutes

What you need: Materials used in earlier lessons, reflection sheets.

Checkpoint task

In small groups, design a small classroom activity (e.g., a route planner or flowchart-driven game). Provide steps, test with another group, and collect feedback.

Self-assessment

Students complete a short checklist: I can decompose a task; I can find patterns; I can write clear steps; I can test and improve.

Next steps

Use this checkpoint to decide which students need extra practice and which can lead peer activities.
