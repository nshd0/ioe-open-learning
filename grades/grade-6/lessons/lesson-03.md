# Lesson 03 — Efficiency & Optimization

Age: 11–12 | Duration: 50 minutes

Learning objectives
- Compare different algorithms for the same task and reason about efficiency (steps, repetition).

VARK differentiation
- Visual: Compare step counts and diagrams side-by-side.
- Auditory: Debate which solution is better and why.
- Read/Write: Write short justification for chosen approach.
- Kinesthetic: Time physical execution of alternative strategies.

Assessment
- Students present two solutions and explain which is preferable with evidence.
