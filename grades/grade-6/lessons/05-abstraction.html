Lesson 05 — Abstraction

Learning objective: Create a simplified model of a real-world situation that keeps important details and hides irrelevant ones.

Time: 30–45 minutes

What you need: Paper, pens, example scenarios.

Big idea

Abstraction helps us focus on what matters for solving a problem while ignoring unimportant details.

Try it

Students map a route through school but only keep steps important for a poster (e.g., turns and landmarks), discarding irrelevant small actions.

Reflection

What did you remove from the full description? Did the simplified version still work?
