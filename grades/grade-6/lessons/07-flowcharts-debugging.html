Lesson 07 — Flowcharts & Debugging

Learning objective: Represent algorithms with simple flowcharts and practice debugging through testing.

Time: 45–60 minutes

What you need: Flowchart symbols sheet, sticky notes, pens.

Big idea

Flowcharts visualise steps and decisions; debugging finds and fixes errors in our steps.

Try it

Create a flowchart for making a simple snack. Exchange with another group and follow their flowchart to see if it works. Record where it failed and propose fixes.

Reflection

What kinds of errors did you find? Were they ordering mistakes, missing steps, or unclear decisions?
