# Lesson 06 — Project: Algorithm Challenge

Age: 11–12 | Duration: 60 minutes

Project brief
- Teams solve a real-world style routing or sorting challenge by designing and testing an algorithm.

VARK differentiation
- Visual: Provide planning templates and success criteria.
- Auditory: Teams present strategy and reasoning.
- Read/Write: Teams document algorithm and test cases.
- Kinesthetic: Use manipulatives to simulate algorithm steps physically.

Assessment
- Rubric: correctness, efficiency, teamwork, explanation.
