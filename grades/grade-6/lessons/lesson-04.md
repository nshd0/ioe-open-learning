# Lesson 04 — Data Representation

Age: 11–12 | Duration: 50 minutes

Learning objectives
- Understand simple ways to record and represent information (tables, lists).

Activities
- Capture routes, scores or steps in simple data tables; discuss why structure matters.

VARK differentiation
- Visual: Provide table templates and sample data.
- Auditory: Read data aloud and discuss patterns.
- Read/Write: Students create and annotate their data tables.
- Kinesthetic: Physically order data cards and create a class table.

Assessment
- Submit a labeled data table and short reflection on its usefulness.
