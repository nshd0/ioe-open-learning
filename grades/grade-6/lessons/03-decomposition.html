Lesson 03 — Decomposition

Learning objective: Break a complex task into manageable steps with clear responsibilities.

Time: 40–50 minutes

What you need: Paper, scissors, large chart paper.

Big idea

Decomposition makes difficult tasks easier by focusing on small, testable parts.

Try it

Students decompose the process of organising a classroom library into steps and assign roles. Test by having another group follow the steps.

Reflection

Which steps needed more detail? How did testing reveal missing steps?
