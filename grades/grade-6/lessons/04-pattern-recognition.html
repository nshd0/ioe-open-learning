Lesson 04 — Pattern Recognition

Learning objective: Identify repeating patterns and useful similarities to simplify tasks.

Time: 30–45 minutes

What you need: Counters, shapes, grid paper.

Big idea

Finding patterns helps us predict outcomes and reuse solutions.

Try it

Students examine class data (arrival times, book choices) to find patterns and suggest a simple rule.

Reflection

What pattern did you find? How might it help make a plan easier?
