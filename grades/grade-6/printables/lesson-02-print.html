Lesson 02 — Stepwise Refinement

Age: 11–12 | Duration: 50 minutes

Learning objectives

  • Break complex problems into smaller, testable steps.

Activities

  • Iteratively refine a messy solution into clear sub-procedures.

VARK differentiation

  • Visual: Show refinement steps in layered diagrams.
  • Auditory: Pair narrate refinement choices.
  • Read/Write: Document each refinement step and test case.
  • Kinesthetic: Physically rearrange steps on cards to represent refinement.

Assessment

  • Submit refined plan with test cases and trace results.
