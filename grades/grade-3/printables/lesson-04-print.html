Lesson 04 — Patterns and Repeats

Age: 8–9 | Duration: 45 minutes

Learning objectives

  • Recognise simple repeating patterns and express them as repeated commands.

Lesson flow

1. Explore visual patterns on grids (10 min).

2. Create a repeating step sequence that draws a pattern (20 min).

3. Share and explain the repeat used (15 min).

Differentiation — VARK

  • Visual: Pattern cards and colour-coded repeat markers.
  • Auditory: Chant repeated sequences in rhythm.
  • Read/Write: Students write a short description of the repeat rule.
  • Kinesthetic: Use clap/step patterns to represent repeats.

Assessment

  • Students demonstrate a repeat and describe the rule in one sentence.
