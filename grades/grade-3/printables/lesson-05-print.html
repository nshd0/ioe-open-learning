Lesson 05 — Simple Procedures

Age: 8–9 | Duration: 50 minutes

Learning objectives

  • Group sequences into named procedures and call them.

Lesson flow

1. Demonstrate creating a short procedure (e.g., "GoToDoor") (10 min).

2. Students design two small procedures and call them from a main sequence (30 min).

3. Share and test (10 min).

Differentiation — VARK

  • Visual: Provide flow-diagram templates to name and call procedures.
  • Auditory: Pair-share the procedure names and steps verbally.
  • Read/Write: Write procedure definitions using a template.
  • Kinesthetic: Act out a named procedure as a group.

Assessment

  • Students produce named procedures and demonstrate composition.
