# Lesson 02 — Step Sequences

Age: 8–9 | Duration: 45 minutes

Learning objectives
- Break a simple task into ordered steps.

Lesson flow
1. Review directional words (5 min).
2. Group task: Plan steps to move from Start to Goal (15 min).
3. Students record steps and test on grid (20 min).

Differentiation — VARK
- Visual: Sequence flowcharts with icons.
- Auditory: Narrate steps aloud and ask peers to repeat.
- Read/Write: Provide step templates and sentence starters.
- Kinesthetic: Use body-movement sequencing (clap, jump, turn).

Assessment
- Students submit written sequences; rubric checks order and clarity.
