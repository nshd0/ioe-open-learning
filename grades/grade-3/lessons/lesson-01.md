# Lesson 01 — Directions & Commands

Age: 8–9 | Duration: 40–50 minutes

Learning objectives
- Understand basic directional vocabulary (forward, back, left, right).
- Translate simple instructions into movement commands.

Outcomes
- Students can follow and give a 4-step sequence of directions.

Materials
- Printed grid (student sheet), counters, arrow cards.

Lesson flow

1. Hook (5 min): Quick Simon Says with directional words.
2. Teach (10 min): Model commands using a classroom token on a grid.
3. Guided practice (15 min): Students plan paths and write commands.
4. Independent/Pair work (10 min): Partners exchange command sets and test.

Differentiation — VARK
- Visual: Show arrows, coloured grids, large printed cards. Provide worked examples with diagrams.
- Auditory: Use call-and-response chants for commands; oral sequencing games.
- Read/Write: Provide sentence strips and written command templates to fill in.
- Kinesthetic: Students physically step along a taped grid on the floor acting as the robot.

Assessment
- Observe pairs execute and correct sequences; checklist: uses correct directions, 4-step sequence.

Accessibility notes
- Use high-contrast printouts and tactile markers for visually impaired students. Offer one-on-one verbal prompts.
