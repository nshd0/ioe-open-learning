# Lesson 06 — Creative Robot Challenges

Age: 8–9 | Duration: 60 minutes

Learning objectives
- Apply sequencing, repeats and procedures to solve open-ended challenges.

Lesson flow
1. Present challenge cards with obstacles and goals (10 min).
2. Teams plan and program a solution using procedures and repeats (35 min).
3. Gallery walk and reflection (15 min).

Differentiation — VARK
- Visual: Large challenge boards and example solutions.
- Auditory: Teams explain strategy aloud during gallery walk.
- Read/Write: Teams write a short plan and commands for submission.
- Kinesthetic: Use floor grids and act out solutions with a student as robot.

Assessment
- Rubric: Correctness, use of procedures/repeats, collaboration, explanation.
