Lesson 04 — Combining Conditions

Age: 10–11 | Duration: 55 minutes

Learning objectives

  • Combine conditions using AND/OR reasoning in simple tasks.

VARK differentiation

  • Visual: Venn-like diagrams to show combined conditions.
  • Auditory: Explain differences between AND/OR with examples.
  • Read/Write: Write compound condition statements.
  • Kinesthetic: Two-trigger games where both or either triggers are required.

Assessment

  • Short quiz of 6 items requiring identification of AND vs OR.
