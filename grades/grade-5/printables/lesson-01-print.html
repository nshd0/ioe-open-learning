Lesson 01 — If/Then Thinking

Age: 10–11 | Duration: 45 minutes

Learning objectives

  • Understand simple conditional reasoning: if X then do Y.

VARK differentiation

  • Visual: Flowchart cards for simple conditions.
  • Auditory: Roleplay conditional dialogues.
  • Read/Write: Fill in conditional sentence frames.
  • Kinesthetic: Physical branching paths where students choose routes by a condition.

Activity

  • Create simple rules for a paper robot that react to a tile colour.

Assessment

  • Students write two conditional rules and demonstrate them.
