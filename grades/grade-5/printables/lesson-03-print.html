Lesson 03 — Sensors & Inputs

Age: 10–11 | Duration: 50 minutes

Learning objectives

  • Understand inputs and how they affect decisions (e.g., colour, distance).

Activities

  • Use simple sensors or teacher-provided cues (cards) to simulate inputs.

VARK differentiation

  • Visual: Sensor/colour cards with labeled triggers.
  • Auditory: Listen to prompts and respond with chosen branches.
  • Read/Write: Create input-to-action tables.
  • Kinesthetic: Students act as sensors and trigger actions when prompted.

Assessment

  • Students map inputs to correct actions and test scenarios.
