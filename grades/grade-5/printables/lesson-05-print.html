Lesson 05 — Debugging Branches

Age: 10–11 | Duration: 50 minutes

Learning objectives

  • Identify incorrect branches and fix logic errors in decision trees.

VARK differentiation

  • Visual: Highlight wrong branches on diagrams.
  • Auditory: Partner talk-through to find logic errors.
  • Read/Write: Annotate a flowchart and correct pseudocode.
  • Kinesthetic: Follow a wrong branch physically and then repair it.

Assessment

  • Diagnostic task: students correct a faulty branching program and explain fix.
