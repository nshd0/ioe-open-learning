Lesson 01 — Functions & Procedures

Age: 13–14 | Duration: 50 minutes

Learning objectives

  • Understand functions as named blocks with inputs and outputs.

VARK differentiation

  • Visual: Block diagrams showing inputs, body, output.
  • Auditory: Explain a function's behaviour to peers.
  • Read/Write: Write function templates and sample calls.
  • Kinesthetic: Group activity where students pass data tokens through "function stations".

Assessment

  • Short task: design a function to compute a simple result and test it.
