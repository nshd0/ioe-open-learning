# Lesson 03 — Decomposition & Reuse

Age: 13–14 | Duration: 55 minutes

Learning objectives
- Break problems into reusable modules and explain reuse benefits.

VARK differentiation
- Visual: Module diagrams and dependency trees.
- Auditory: Group discussion about reuse cases.
- Read/Write: Document module interfaces and examples of reuse.
- Kinesthetic: Compose a larger task from smaller physical modules.

Assessment
- Provide a decomposition plan and reuse points for a sample problem.
