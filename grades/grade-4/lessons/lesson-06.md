# Lesson 06 — Build-a-Pattern Project

Age: 9–10 | Duration: 60 minutes

Learning objectives
- Apply loops and nesting to produce a final design; collaborate on planning and coding the pattern.

Project brief
- Teams produce a decorative band or board using loops; prepare a short presentation describing choices.

VARK differentiation
- Visual: Provide templates and colour palettes.
- Auditory: Teams rehearse oral presentation and explanation.
- Read/Write: Produce written algorithm and step comments.
- Kinesthetic: Build a large floor pattern with repeating blocks.

Assessment
- Rubric: algorithmic correctness, creativity, teamwork, explanation.
