Lesson 01 — Observing Patterns

Age: 9–10 | Duration: 40 minutes

Learning objectives

  • Identify and describe repeating and growing patterns.

VARK differentiation

  • Visual: Pattern strips and colour sequences.
  • Auditory: Pattern chants and clapping sequences.
  • Read/Write: Describe rules in words and number sequences.
  • Kinesthetic: Create human patterns using movement.

Lesson activities

  • Explore physical and visual pattern examples; students create pattern cards and describe the rule.

Assessment

  • Exit ticket: draw a pattern and write the rule.
