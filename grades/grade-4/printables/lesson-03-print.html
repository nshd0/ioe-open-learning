Lesson 03 — Looping Actions

Age: 9–10 | Duration: 50 minutes

Learning objectives

  • Create looped actions to reduce repetition in sequences.

Activities

  • Use blocks or tiles to build actions; refactor long sequences into loops.

VARK differentiation

  • Visual: Show before/after diagrams of loop refactoring.
  • Auditory: Group discussion on why loops reduce errors.
  • Read/Write: Students rewrite sequences using loop notation.
  • Kinesthetic: Team relay where students perform the body actions for a loop.

Assessment

  • Short task: refactor a 12-step sequence into a loop with proper count.
