Lesson 06 — Mini Data Project

Age: 12–13 | Duration: 60 minutes

Project brief

  • Collect a small dataset (e.g., class favourites), store it in lists, and answer questions using search and aggregation.

VARK differentiation

  • Visual: Provide templates and sample charts.
  • Auditory: Present findings to class orally.
  • Read/Write: Submit data-handling steps and short write-up.
  • Kinesthetic: Use physical counters to represent counts and aggregates.

Assessment

  • Rubric: correctness of data, use of lists, clarity of explanation.
