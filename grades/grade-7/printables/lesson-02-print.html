Lesson 02 — Storing and Updating Data

Age: 12–13 | Duration: 50 minutes

Learning objectives

  • Read and update variables; understand assignment and change over time.

VARK differentiation

  • Visual: Timelines showing variable values across steps.
  • Auditory: Explain sequence of assignments to a peer.
  • Read/Write: Complete code-like assignment exercises.
  • Kinesthetic: Use number cards to update an on-class display variable.

Assessment

  • Task: predict final value after a sequence of updates.
