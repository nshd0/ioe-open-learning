# Grade 6 — Logic Lab

Grade profile: ages 11–12. Focus on algorithms, flowcharts, and debugging with tangible prototypes.

Year-level learning goals:
- Design simple algorithms and represent them with flowcharts.
- Test and refine algorithms using examples.

Unit sequence:
1. Computational Thinking in Action
2. Flowchart Builders
3. Debugging Lab
4. Hello AI

Core vocabulary: algorithm, flowchart, bug, test, refine.

Suggested duration: 7–9 weeks.

Unplugged option: paper-based flowchart exercises and role-play debugging.

Artifact: Scratch-style quiz or story plan (low-tech storyboard).

Formative evidence: flowchart sketches, bug reports, teacher rubric.

Inclusion: pair programming analogues, visual step checklists, extended time.

Parent/home connection: describe an everyday routine as a sequence of steps and test with a family member.

Responsible-AI expectations: introduce simple examples of AI as pattern recognisers; emphasise limits and verification.
