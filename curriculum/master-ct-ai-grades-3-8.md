# Master CT & AI progression (Grades 3–8)

This master document summarises the learning progression across Grades 3–8 for Computational Thinking (CT) and introductory Artificial Intelligence (AI). Use the grade-level files for classroom-ready guidance.

- Aim: build observable skills (pattern spotting, decomposition, algorithms, data literacy, responsible AI judgement).
- Assessment principle: formative evidence collected through artifacts, checklists, and short reflections.

See the grade-specific files in this folder for full templates and unit-level outcomes.
