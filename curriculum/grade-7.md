# Grade 7 — Data Studio

Grade profile: ages 12–13. Emphasise data collection, classification, and simple visualisation; introduce fairness and bias at a conceptual level.

Year-level learning goals:
- Collect and clean simple datasets.
- Create basic charts and summaries to tell data stories.

Unit sequence:
1. Data Detectives
2. Data Stories
3. Language and Machines
4. Fair or Flawed?

Core vocabulary: data, label, class, bias, visualisation.

Suggested duration: 7–9 weeks.

Unplugged option: paper-based surveys, card sorting, and tally charts.

Artifact: Classroom Data Story — a small presentation with visuals.

Formative evidence: collected datasets, charts, reflection on fairness.

Inclusion: visual aids, multiple representations, support for multilingual learners.

Parent/home connection: simple household survey and shared chart.

Responsible-AI expectations: discuss how data shapes outcomes and why fairness matters.
