# Grade 5 — Map Lab

Grade profile: ages 10–11. Introduce simple inputs/outputs and decision-making.

Year-level learning goals:
- Abstract real situations into simple maps or decision cards.
- Test choices and observe outcomes.

Unit sequence:
1. Map Makers
2. Choice Machines
3. Inputs and Outputs
4. Decision Debuggers

Core vocabulary: abstraction, input, output, condition, decision.

Suggested duration: 6–8 weeks.

Unplugged option: route-mapping with classroom maps and choice cards.

Artifact: Smart Classroom Decision Board.

Formative evidence: decision logs, paired tests, reflective notes.

Inclusion: clear visuals, scaffolded prompts, low-tech alternatives.

Parent/home connection: map a route from home to a nearby shop and discuss choices.

Responsible-AI expectations: introduce the idea of tools that make suggestions; emphasise human judgement.
