# Grade 8 — AI Futures

Grade profile: ages 13–14. Deeper investigation into systems, data quality, and responsible AI; project-based capstone.

Year-level learning goals:
- Understand data quality and simple ML ideas.
- Design a small prototype that applies CT and responsible-AI thinking.

Unit sequence:
1. Systems Thinkers
2. Data and Machine Learning
3. Generative AI with Judgement
4. Responsible AI
5. AI for a Better School

Core vocabulary: model, training, validation, privacy, accountability.

Suggested duration: 8–12 weeks.

Unplugged option: role-play datasets, paper-based model-building and evaluation.

Artifact: AI for a Better School capstone prototype and presentation.

Formative evidence: prototype plan, evaluation checklist, peer review.

Inclusion: scaffolded data examples, accessible presentation formats, clear ethical prompts.

Parent/home connection: discuss how data is used in everyday apps and what checks people should make.

Responsible-AI expectations: teach verification, attribution, data minimisation, and fairness checks.
